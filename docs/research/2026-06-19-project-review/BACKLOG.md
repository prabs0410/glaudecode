# GlaudeCode — Prioritized Backlog

> Produced by a multi-agent review (gaps · edge-cases · bugs · security · performance · architecture-debt · test-coverage · ux · observability · dx) on 2026-06-19, cross-referenced against competitor analysis and evidenced market pain-points.

This backlog ranks every confirmed finding by impact-per-effort, weighted toward the product's stated thesis: a **private, full-fidelity Claude Code cockpit on the user's own Mac + Tailscale**, driven **lid-closed and voice-first**, where **silent failure is the enemy**. Items that block that core promise (away-mode reachability, push, approval correctness, observability of phone failures) rank above polish.

## Notes on ranking

- **Observability fixes (#1, #2) lead** despite being small because they are force-multipliers: they make every other failure in the system visible to the lid-closed founder, which is the explicit product mandate.
- **#3, #4, #6, #7/#8 are the correctness/safety core** of the away-mode promise — a wrong approval, a lockout on respawn, or a misrouted keystroke each break trust in a way the competitor analysis shows is the category's whole reason to exist (Anthropic's own Remote Control fails exactly here on approval-desync and reconnect).
- **#40 (unmerged branches) is the single biggest maintainability liability** but is gated on a human gh-auth action, so it sits below the engineering work the team can do unblocked today.
- Several performance items (#13, #33) collapse into one shared-cache change; do #13 first and re-measure before investing in #33/#37.
- WS4 (#7) **must not ship without** its threat-model and device-bound-token hardening (#8) — they are one unit of work, not two independent tickets.
